# Reveal

Applies the system's motion presets to any element.

- `mode="load"` with `index`: the hero entrance, blur plus 14px rise, staggered 90ms. Use once per page.
- `mode="scroll"` (default): fades from 25% and rises 24px as the element enters the viewport.
- `mode="tilt"`: a product window that flattens from an 18° tilt as it scrolls in; give the parent `perspective: 1600px`.
- Everything ends at the resting state, so content is complete with reduced motion or no JavaScript.
