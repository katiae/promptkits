# Typography

`Heading`, `Text` and `Eyebrow` apply the type scale so screens never set sizes by hand.

- `Heading` level 1 to 4 maps to display-lg, display-md, display-sm and card-title. Override the look with `size` when the outline needs a different level.
- Headings are always Geist 600 in solid `hy-fg`, with tight negative tracking and balanced wrapping. No gradients, serif or italic words.
- `Text` defaults to body in `hy-fg-2`; `size="lede"` for intros; `mono` for IDs and numbers.
- `Eyebrow` labels a section: mono, uppercase, `hy-signal`, after an 18px rule.
